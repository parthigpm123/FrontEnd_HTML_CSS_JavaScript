A paragraph is a distinct, self-contained unit of writing comprising one or more sentences that develop a single, central idea. It acts as a structural, cohesive building block in essays and reports, usually starting on a new line and sometimes indented. A strong paragraph typically includes a topic sentence, supporting details, and a concluding sentence.
